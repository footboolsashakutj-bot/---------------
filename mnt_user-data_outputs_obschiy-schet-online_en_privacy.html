Privacy Policy

What data we store

The group name, participant names and expenses (description, amount, who paid) that you enter yourself. There are no accounts: we don’t ask for an email, phone number or password. Please don’t enter sensitive information.

Where data is stored

Group data is stored in the Supabase cloud database. Anyone who has a group’s link can access that group. Don’t post the link publicly.

Browser storage

Your browser remembers the ID of the last group you opened so it can take you back to it. This is needed for the service to work. You can clear it with the “Leave group” button or in your browser settings.

Ads and analytics

The service currently shows no ads and uses no analytics. If that changes, this page will be updated and consent will be requested where required.

Deleting data

To delete a group and all its data, email [YOUR EMAIL] with the group link.
