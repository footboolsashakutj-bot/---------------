Shared Tab

Create a group and send the link to your friends: everyone sees and adds expenses in one place.

How it works

  1. Create a group and add the people.
  2. Log expenses: who paid and for whom.
  3. Send friends the link: everyone sees the balances and can add expenses.

What it’s for

Trips, shared rent, parties, group gifts. The app works out who owes whom and reduces it to the fewest transfers. Free, no sign-up.
